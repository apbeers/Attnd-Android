Attnd is the companion app for the event you're at. Scan the event's QR code and everything the organizers publish is on your phone: where things are, what's on and the latest news.

Map of the whole event
Every stage, vendor, food truck, restroom and first aid point the organizers have added is pinned on one map. Tap a pin for details, or show your own location to find your way.

Schedule with now and next
See what's happening right now and what's coming up next, and search the full program. Times are always shown in the event's own time zone, so they match the printed program even if your phone is set to another zone.

Locations and details
Look up any spot at the event: its description and photo, the address, walking directions in your maps app, and everything scheduled there.

Updates from the organizers
Set time changes, weather notices and announcements appear in the Updates tab. Turn on notifications in Options if you'd like them on your lock screen as well.

Always up to date
When organizers change the map or the schedule, the app picks up the change within seconds while it's open. The last version is kept on your phone, so you can still check it when the signal at the venue drops.

Join in seconds
Scan the QR code on a poster, ticket or wristband, type the event code, or look for events near you. There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Attnd never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

Made for phones and foldables
On a foldable, open it up to see the map beside the schedule or the list of places.

Organizing an event? Create your event and its map at attnd.app.
