Attnd is the companion app for the event you're at: a festival, a conference, a fair or a market. Scan the event's QR code and everything the organizers publish is right there: where things are, what's on and the latest news.

Join in seconds
Scan the QR code on a poster, ticket or wristband, type the event code, or look for events near you. There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

Home at a glance
What's on now and next, the organizers' latest announcement and the plans you've made for the day, all on one screen.

The whole event on one map
Every stage, vendor, food truck, restroom and first aid point the organizers have added is pinned on one map. Tap a pin to see what's on there, how far it is to walk and the route to get there.

Schedule with now and next
See what's happening right now and what's coming up, with countdowns, and search the full program. Times are always shown in the event's own time zone, so they match the printed program.

My Schedule
Tap the heart on a set, a talk or a place to add it to My Schedule. Give a food truck a time, choose when to be reminded, and see the day laid out with the walks in between. If the organizers move something you saved, Attnd tells you.

On your lock screen
Follow the day in a notification that shows what's on now and what's next, and get a reminder with the walking time before each thing in My Schedule. On Android 16 it appears as a Live Update.

Ask Attnd
On phones with Gemini Nano, ask about the event in plain words or have Attnd plan your afternoon around the music and food you like. It runs on the device, so questions never leave it.

Updates from the organizers
Set time changes, weather notices and announcements appear in Updates, and urgent ones come through even if you've turned other notifications off.

Works when the signal doesn't
When organizers change the map or the schedule, the app picks up the change within seconds. The latest version and its photos are kept on the phone, so everything still works when the signal at the venue drops.

Private by design
Attnd never asks for your name, email address or phone number. Location and notifications are optional.

Made for phones and foldables
On a foldable, open it up to see the map beside the schedule or the list of places.

Organizing an event? Create your event and its map at attnd.app.
